🛍️ NBSSY — E-Commerce Website
A simple fashion e-commerce landing page for exploring and shopping unique clothes and accessories.
🔗 Live Demo: nbssy-e-commerce-website.vercel.app
Features

Hero section with a "Shop Now" call-to-action
Trending products showcase with prices and cart buttons
Seasonal sale banners (Winter, Spring/Summer collections)
Customer testimonials section
Multi-page layout: Home, About Us, Store, Contact
Wishlist and cart icons in the navbar
Social media links (Instagram, Twitter, Snapchat, Facebook, YouTube)
